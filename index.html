Research

01 · Molecular Dynamics

Predicting conformational changes of proteins and computing their free-energy landscapes.

  • How does a protein move between its functional states, and along which pathway?
  • What does the free-energy landscape along that change look like?
02 · QM/MM

Free-energy calculations between two states of a photoreceptor protein, using QM/MM.

  • What makes one state more stable than the other?
03 · Enzyme Discovery & Engineering

Finding promising enzymes among large numbers of sequences and improving them, using structure prediction and machine-learning models alongside simulation.

  • Which candidates are worth testing in the lab, and which mutations are likely to help?
